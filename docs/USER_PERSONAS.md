# User Personas

## Persona A — "Eat Now" — Ade, 29, works in central Manchester

Hungry, at or near a place to buy food (Tesco Express, a cafe, a takeaway menu), 5–10 minutes of patience. Wants a short, confident answer, not a menu of options to research. Price and distance matter more than novelty. Success = picks something in under a minute and doesn't regret it.

**Primary need:** "What can I eat now, near me, for what I've got to spend?"

## Persona B — "Cook Today" — Priya, 34, cooks for her household most weeknights

At home, has *some* ingredients already, doesn't want to plan a week ahead tonight — just tonight. Time pressure varies (20 minutes some nights, more on others). Wants to use what she has before buying more (§13 Principle 11). Success = a realistic recipe she can actually finish with what's in the kitchen.

**Primary need:** "What can I make with what I already have, in the time I've got?"

## Persona C — "Plan Ahead" — Sam, 41, budget-conscious parent

Wants to get ahead of the week's food decisions and spend, but has been burned by rigid meal-planning apps that fall apart the first time plans change. Values a shopping list that actually matches the plan and a budget that doesn't sneak past target. Success = a flexible plan that still holds up after two changes mid-week.

**Primary need:** "Help me plan without locking myself into something I'll abandon by Wednesday."

## Cross-Cutting Persona Trait

All three personas are UK consumers making everyday, non-medical food decisions. None of them are using this app to manage a diagnosed condition — that user exists, but per Decisions 10-11 the product explicitly does not serve that need, and directs them to a qualified professional instead (§27).

## Anti-Persona (Explicitly Out of Scope for MVP)

A user seeking allergy management, medical diet prescription, or diagnosis. Any user research or feature request pulling toward this persona should be flagged against [RISK_REGISTER.md](RISK_REGISTER.md) R1 before being actioned.
